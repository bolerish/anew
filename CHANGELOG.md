# Min Changelog

## Version v1.2.0

Released on 2026-09-15.

### Bookmark layout and display

- Arrange folders in rows and resizable columns, resize folder heights, and
  double-click a resize handle to reset it.

- Choose list or grid view, icon style, titles and domain visibility for each
  folder. Grid tiles are more compact.

- Keep folders collapsed after reloading or opening a new tab. Dropping an item
  or folder onto itself keeps it in place.

### Editing and saving

- Edit with undo, redo, save and cancel. Close an add form by moving to another
  control.

- Adding the same address to the same folder shows a notification instead of
  creating a duplicate, including bookmarks not yet saved in edit mode.

- Bookmark input and save errors appear below the fields without clearing your
  entries. Repeated submissions are blocked while saving, and location
  searches distinguish loading from errors.

### Search and navigation

- Find bookmarks with search suggestions and choose Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo or
  Brave.

- Use the table of contents to navigate to folders and reveal their names on
  hover or keyboard focus. A clicked item stays highlighted until you manually
  scroll.

- Navigate bookmarks with arrow keys, focus search with /, and enter editing
  with E. Escape leaves search focus and opens settings when idle. Editing
  restores focus, with improved screen-reader guidance.

### Weather

- View weather for a searched city or your location. Choose Celsius or
  Fahrenheit and whether to show the city. Cached weather avoids repeated
  requests.

### Display settings

- Set heading and content fonts separately, adjust content width, and choose
  from five languages. Toggle folder dividers and keyboard shortcuts in
  Layout.

- Keep the header right-aligned on mobile and the settings button on the right
  when shortcuts are hidden.

- Choose whether to show the date and time and select their formats. Time
  options show examples, and custom date formats include examples and error
  guidance.

- Choose light, dark, or system themes, and turn animations on, off, or follow
  system settings. Saved themes are applied before new-tab content loads.

### Update history

- Read update history without leaving the new tab and navigate with browser Back
  and Forward. Unread dots disappear after opening the history.

## Version v1.1.3

Released on 2026-08-25.

- Moved settings from a dropdown menu to a left sidebar.
- Dragging a bookmark now pushes nearby bookmarks aside to preview its new
  position.
- Bookmark folders now expand and collapse as accordions.
- You can now choose nested folders, not just root folders, when saving a
  bookmark.
